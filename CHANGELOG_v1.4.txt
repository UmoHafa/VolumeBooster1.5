Volume Booster v1.4

Primary goal: make Boost audibly effective on more Android 9+ devices.

Changes
- Automatic engine now tries Android DynamicsProcessing first.
- DynamicsProcessing uses its real input-gain stage (0 to +12 dB) and default limiter.
- Uses high effect priority where Android exposes a priority constructor.
- Keeps LoudnessEnhancer and Equalizer as fallbacks.
- Keeps exact-player-session effects when cooperating players announce a session.
- Keeps session 0 only as the compatibility/global fallback.
- Diagnostics report the selected engine and gain readback.

Important Android limitation
Third-party Play Store apps cannot force all other apps to expose their private audio session IDs. Session-0/global processing is deprecated and vendor-dependent, so no non-root implementation can honestly guarantee global boost on every Android phone/app.
